# Compliance Analysis Skill

## Purpose

Identify compliance-related obligations and potential gaps in an agreement.

## Instructions

1. Read the agreement and understand the question.
2. Identify clauses relevant to compliance.
3. Use extracted clauses from other skills when available.
4. Examine:
   - Regulatory obligations
   - Data protection and privacy
   - Information security
   - Audit rights
   - Subcontractor obligations
   - Compliance reporting
5. Explain potential compliance concerns in simple language.
6. Distinguish explicit contractual requirements from missing information.

## Output

For each concern, provide:

- Clause reference
- Compliance concern
- Supporting agreement text
- Potential business impact
- Recommended action

## Rules

- Never invent regulations, obligations, or clauses.
- Do not assume a missing clause means non-compliance.
- If the agreement provides insufficient evidence, say so.
- Do not make definitive legal conclusions.
